Pennly Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Pennly is a personal expense and budget tracker designed with privacy in mind. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it is stored.

In short: Pennly does not sell personal information, use advertising trackers, or send your financial records to external analytics or AI services.

1. Information stored by Pennly

Expenses, budgets, categories, recurring expenses, reminder preferences, and app settings are stored on your device. Pennly does not operate a server that receives or stores your financial records.

2. iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud features, Apple may synchronize your Pennly data through your personal iCloud account so it can be available across your Apple devices. This transfer is handled by Apple's iCloud service. Pennly does not have access to your Apple account or iCloud contents.

3. Predictions and machine learning

Pennly's spending predictions and category assistance use information already stored in the app. Processing is performed on the device. Your expense data is not sent to Pennly, third-party APIs, or external AI services for analysis.

4. Camera and notifications

Pennly requests camera access only when you choose to scan a receipt. Receipt images are used to help fill in expense information and are not sent to Pennly's servers. If you enable reminders or budget alerts, Pennly schedules notifications on your device.

5. Purchases

Pennly uses Apple's StoreKit service to process Premium purchases and restore previous purchases. Apple handles payment details, billing, and receipts. Pennly does not receive or store your credit card number, billing address, or other payment credentials.

6. Analytics, advertising, and sharing

Pennly does not include third-party advertising networks or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share your personal or financial information with third parties.

7. Children's privacy

Pennly does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Pennly does not operate a service that collects personal information, no account is required to use the app.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Pennly's features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
