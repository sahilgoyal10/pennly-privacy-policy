# Privacy Policy for Pennly

**Effective Date:** April 9, 2026

Pennly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. We built Pennly with a strict privacy-first approach. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Pennly application.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
**We do not collect, store, track, or share any of your personal or financial data.** All the data you enter into Pennly—including your expenses, categories, and exported reports—is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your data, and we do not maintain any external servers to store it.

## 2. Multi-Device Sync
If you choose to use Pennly across multiple devices, your data is synced using your personal Apple iCloud account. This synchronisation is handled entirely by Apple's secure infrastructure. We cannot view, access, or manage the data stored in your iCloud. 

## 3. AI Predictions
Pennly utilises artificial intelligence to predict your upcoming monthly expenses. All AI processing is performed locally on your device. Your financial data is **never** sent to any external servers, third-party APIs, or cloud platforms for processing. 

## 4. In-App Purchases and Premium Features
Pennly offers in-app purchases to unlock premium features. All transactions are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect, process, or have access to your payment information (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses). 

## 5. Third-Party Tracking and Analytics
We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking software into Pennly. Your usage habits, spending patterns, and interactions within the app remain completely private.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we inherently do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.
